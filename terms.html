Rental terms

Read this before you confirm your booking.

1. Driver and documents

Bring a valid driving licence and a photo ID to pick-up. Age limits and licence requirements depend on the car.

2. Price and payment

The price is the daily rate multiplied by the number of rental days. Payment and deposit are made at pick-up. No card details are collected online.

3. Reservation and cancellation

A reservation is linked to your account. You can cancel a future reservation from your dashboard. Contact us to change dates or location.

4. Insurance and car condition

Check insurance, fuel rules and mileage limits in the rental agreement. Report any damage or accident right away.
